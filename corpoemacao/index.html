Corpo em Ação

A Flutter workout tracker built for the place it is used: a gym basement with bad signal. Everything works offline first and syncs later, and the camera checks your squat on the phone itself.

Plans adapt to the user's goal and available equipment, sessions run with timers and rest tracking, and progress, body measurements and history are charted. AI suggestions come from an LLM, either with the user's own key or through the project's server with a daily quota.

Architecture

Local first,
cloud when it can.

The phone is the source of truth during a workout; the cloud catches up when the connection comes back.

Engineering decisions

Three calls worth explaining
in an interview.

01 · Offline

The sync indicator never lies.

Every write lands in Hive first and, if it must reach the server, joins a queue that is sent when connectivity returns. The status indicator reads that queue, so it cannot show "all synced" while operations are still pending.

02 · Posture

Rules on top of pose detection.

ML Kit finds the body's landmarks on the device; small evaluators turn them into joint angles and check explicit thresholds, such as a knee angle above 130° meaning the squat was not deep enough.

Rules are easy to explain to a user and to test, and no video leaves the phone.

03 · Cost

One policy for every AI call.

Workout and nutrition suggestions share one execution path: a key passed in tests, then the user's own key, and only then the project's server, which enforces a trial period and a daily limit.

Keeping it in one place avoids the bug where one feature forgets to count the quota.

Limitations

What this project
is not.

  • Posture rules are heuristic thresholds, not validated by a physiotherapist, and cover three exercises.
  • AI workout plans are suggestions from a general LLM, not a prescription from a professional.
  • The repository is private and there is no public build linked here; ask me for a walkthrough on a device.
